TAKEBOOK

Your practice stays yours.

Takebook is local-first. Piano takes, names, notes, folders, and license tokens stay on this device unless you deliberately export or verify a purchase.

Data stored on your device

Takebook stores takes in your browser’s IndexedDB and preferences and an optional teacher-pack license in localStorage. You can delete individual takes, clear your browser data, or export and re-import a JSON backup.

Network requests

The app and its fonts, images, and synthesizer run from this site and work offline after the first visit. If you add a teacher-pack license, the app sends that token to the Sociobot verification endpoint at most once per day. We do not upload recordings.

Payments

Checkout is hosted by Sociobot, with Dodo acting as merchant of record. Their checkout policies apply to information you provide there. Takebook receives only a license token.

Analytics

No behavioral analytics, advertising trackers, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts are included.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
