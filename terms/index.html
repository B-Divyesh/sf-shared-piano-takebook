You may use the free recorder and local exports without an account. You are responsible for your device, hearing-safe volume, and rights to material you record or share.

Teacher pack

The Teacher pack is a one-time purchase that unlocks folders and printable practice sheets for the licensed user. The current price shown in the app is the checkout price. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and handles checkout and refunds. A refund or invalid license revokes the unlock. Do not share or resell a token.
